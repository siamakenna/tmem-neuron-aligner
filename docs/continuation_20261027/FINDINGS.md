# What the diagnostic and review infrastructure establish

The completed diagnostic panel covers 12 selected neurons nested in six wells and 108 repeated visits. These are not 108 independent biological replicates. Historical three-case diagnostics remain regression controls, not additional accepted samples.

## Preserved observations

Primary valid 488 flags remain 91/108, with 17 failed visit gates. Six tracks are diagnostically rejected and six remain pending independent review; none are scientifically accepted. Informational soma-screen passes do not override visit failures or resolve ROI ownership.

Changing area=180 to area=220 increased valid flags from 91 to 97 in the completed sensitivity run, but also changed masks/cores. This is parameter sensitivity, not six recovered biological observations or improved accuracy. The alternative remains unadopted. Tracking proposal displacement and mask overlap measure agreement/sensitivity, not identity-error or contamination rates.

Case-specific areas, displacements, validity reasons, geometry, mask agreement and provisional neighboring-object concerns belong in private evidence. Original detailed receipts and the original case-triage handoff were preserved with SHA256 references, not rewritten as new measurements.

## New practical outputs

The offline workspace displays existing fixed-crop and larger registered-search-context sheets by neuron/day. A private readiness summary identifies each visit's immutable automated failures and links decisions to exact source/display/trace hashes. Blank human annotations remain blank; uncertainty and negative decisions are retained explicitly.

The validator distinguishes structural completeness from unresolved/negative decisions and final scientific eligibility. An all-positive 488 sheet may only become ELIGIBLE_FOR_EXISTING_FINAL_REVIEW when its current source/QC requirements pass. This is not an approval or an export.

No tracking/segmentation accuracy, contamination rate or identity-switch frequency is reported because independent source-bound ground-truth labels have not been supplied. Provisional agent triage is not independent human review.

## Reproducibility and privacy

Selection is now bound to an explicit versioned roster/seed/input checksums/selected order, rather than arbitrary Markdown contents. The historical panel is not called held out.

Private source-specific receipts are represented publicly by their original hashes. Public aggregates above are retained deliberately for checkpoint continuity, not simply because they are small. No reviewer records, galleries, arrays, raw data, environments or detailed logs belong in the PR.
